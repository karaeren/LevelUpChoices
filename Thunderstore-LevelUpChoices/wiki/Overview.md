# Mod Overview

LevelUpChoices changes how you get items. Instead of finding chests and printers, you earn items by leveling up and choosing your desired items.

Every time your team levels up, you get a choice of items. You can pick one item from the selection.

Prettty much anything is configurable. Including the amount of choices you get, the rerolls, banishes you get, tiers of the items, etc... Also almost all configs work in realtime meaning you don't have to restart the run or the game to apply them.

## How to play

You can enable the mod by using the Artifact of Choice when starting the game. You can also turn the "always enabled" option in the config file. If the mod is active, chests and other interactables will not spawn on the stage (configurable). You must rely on your levels to grow stronger.

When you level up, you can press the configured key (F3 by default) to open up the item selection menu. If also configured, the game will pause too (works on multiplayer as well).

## Compatibility

The mod works in singleplayer and multiplayer. All players must have the mod installed to play together. It works with most other mods and custom items. LookingGlass is highly recommended for better item descriptions.
