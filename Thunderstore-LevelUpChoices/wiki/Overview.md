# Mod Overview

LevelUpChoices changes how you get items. You get a pick every time your team levels up, and most chests and printers are gone while interactable removal is on.

Equipment first shows up on your 10th round by default. You can hit **Choose Item Instead** if you want an item that round.

## How to play

Turn on the Artifact of Choice when starting a run. You can also turn on **Always Enable Mod** in Risk of Options.

Once you've leveled up, press **F3** to open the menu. You can change the key in the settings. If you want the game to pause while picking in multiplayer, the host needs to turn on multiplayer pause.

## Compatibility

Everyone in multiplayer needs the same version of the mod. LookingGlass is recommended if you want more info on the cards.
